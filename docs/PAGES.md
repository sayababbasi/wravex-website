# Pages Architecture

## Global Layout
- Max content width: 1200-1280px
- Horizontal padding: 32-48px (Desktop), 16-20px (Mobile)
- Vertical section spacing: 80-120px

## Homepage (/)
Sections: Hero, Intro/Brand, Services, Solutions, Why Wravex, Featured Projects, Technology, Process, Testimonials, CTA.

## Services (/services)
Detailed service presentations. Individual pages for `/services/ai-solutions`, `/services/web-development`, etc.

## Projects (/projects)
Case study listings and individual project pages (`/projects/[slug]`).

## About (/about)
Sections: Story, Mission, Vision, Values.

## Contact (/contact)
Forms with proper validation (Name, Email, Service, Project Details).
